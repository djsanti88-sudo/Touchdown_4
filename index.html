Our impact

Every adoption opens space for another animal. Foster families offer a calm place to recover, and volunteers help pets get ready for their next chapter.

The following figures are sample content for this class project, not verified rescue statistics.

  • 120 pets placed in adoptive homes during the sample year.
  • 35 foster households providing temporary care.
  • 60 volunteers supporting animal care and community events.

How you can help

  • Learn how adoption works.
  • Explore fostering if you have room at home.
  • Ask about volunteering with animals or events.
