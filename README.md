# Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Touchstone 4 adds interactive guidance, form validation, and saved browser data to the rescue website.

[View the website](https://djsanti88-sudo.github.io/Touchdown_4/)

The four pages cover the rescue’s purpose, ways to help, background, and contact information. They include the supplied images, audio, and video.

On Services, visitors can choose Volunteer, Foster, or Adoption to see guidance and carry their choice into the contact form. The form displays validation feedback beside each field and restores a saved draft after a refresh in the same tab.

The contact form is a demonstration and does not send inquiries. Sample impact figures and office hours are identified on the website.
